Adaptation: scoped class names, removed editor icons/debugger/metrics; included Leaf
expression base. Product-driven tree ticking and delta-based interruptible waits.
Health owns enemy HP; hitboxes deduplicate DamageSource instance per victim.
Melee retains signal gating, attack instances and interruption. The scoped
BaseEnemy/HybridEnemy, CharacterAnimations recipients and Patrol/EnemyActions
trees coordinate the retained Orc rig through an actual AnimationTree. Product
adapters own teaching/defense policy, player HP, persistence and reward facts;
the reusable enemy owns enemy motion, animation, health and posed contact.
Enemy target/health/death wiring is adapted without Globals or root motion.
Navigation polygons sample existing authored terrain and occupied volumes;
no demo map, navmesh or collision floor is loaded.
No Blender/Mixamo/Kenney/CynicMusic assets or unqualified template audio copied.
Existing Orc/Ranger/Forest and feedback resources retain their existing notices.
