Adaptation: scoped class names, removed editor icons/debugger/metrics; included Leaf
expression base. Product-driven tree ticking and delta-based interruptible waits.
Health owns enemy HP; hitboxes deduplicate DamageSource instance per victim.
Melee retains signal gating, attack instances and interruption; AnimationPlayer
method events replace demo AnimationTree blending. Product adapters own defense,
player HP, persistence, physics and existing actor presentation. Enemy's blackboard
target/health/death wiring is adapted without Globals or root motion. The small
Forest encounter uses collision/ground-checked steering; no demo navmesh is loaded.
No Blender/Mixamo/Kenney/CynicMusic assets or unqualified template audio copied.
Existing Orc/Ranger/Forest and feedback resources retain their existing notices.
