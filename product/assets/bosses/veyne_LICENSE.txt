The original registered sample receipt, original FBX files, atlas, SOURCE.md
and License.txt are retained in veyne_monster_source. Its original folder
notice misnames Ultimate Platformer Pack; the original SOURCE.md identifies
the official Ultimate Monsters page and explains that discrepancy.

The generated veyne_animations.glb carries the native Demon mesh, complete
skeleton, skin, trident, embedded atlas/material and fourteen native actions.
veyne_export_source.py records the reproducible Blender 4.5 conversion.
Blender export selects and normalizes the four largest skin weights where
the original FBX has more than four; this requires native pose review.

Product adaptation: private animation aliases and loop flags, horizontal
root anchoring, native raised-left-arm/staff Wave for Cast, native No gesture for Reveal,
and LowerArm.R/Middle1.R contact for Punch. The trident remains natively
skinned to Middle1.L. No third-party movement, player, health or save owner
is imported with the presentation.

Native local visual forward: +Z after the Blender glTF Y-up conversion,
as measured from both native foot-rest toe directions (-Y in Blender).
Native scale is normalized by the existing product enemy adapter.
Runtime import, rendered poses, arena clearance and quality remain subject
to product qualification; source presence does not establish acceptance.
